GTD Brain Dump

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how GTD Brain Dump (“the app”) handles information when you use the app at jayeshbhagat7.github.io/braindump.

1. Information you enter

The app is a personal task and project manager. Depending on how you use it, information you enter may include task titles and notes, projects, sections, labels, priorities, due dates and times, deadlines, durations, comments, recurring-task settings, goal information, and task status or organization details.

2. Where information is stored

  • Your browser: The app uses browser local storage to retain app data and sync-related settings on your device. Data may remain in that browser until you delete it or clear the browser’s site data.
  • Google Drive: If you connect Google, the app can save and retrieve task data in the app-specific appDataFolder area of your Google Drive. This is intended for app data and cross-device synchronization.
  • Google Calendar: If Google access is enabled and you assign dates to tasks, the app can create corresponding all-day events on your primary calendar. When tasks are completed or deleted, the app attempts to remove their associated events.

The app is a client-side web app and does not operate its own application server to store your task content. GitHub Pages hosts the website files. Google processes information handled by its services under Google's applicable terms and privacy policies.

3. Google account access

If you choose to connect a Google account, the app requests these Google OAuth scopes:

  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/calendar.events — to create and delete calendar events associated with your dated tasks.
  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.appdata — to read and write the app's data in your Google Drive app-specific data folder.

The app uses this access to provide the sync features described above. It does not sell Google user data or use it for advertising. It does not intentionally share your task content with the app developer or other third parties through an app-operated server.

4. Sharing and disclosure

The app does not sell your personal information. Task content is stored in your browser and, if you enable sync, in the Google services you authorize. Information may be processed by Google as necessary to provide those services, subject to Google's own terms and policies. The app does not intentionally disclose task content to unrelated third parties.

5. Retention and deletion

Your browser-stored information remains on your device until you remove it, for example by using available app controls or clearing site data in your browser. Synced information may remain in your Google Drive app data folder until removed through the app or otherwise deleted from your Google account. Calendar events created by the app may remain in Google Calendar if an attempted deletion does not succeed; check your calendar directly if needed.

You can also revoke the app's Google account access in your Google Account's third-party connections settings. Revoking access prevents future authorized API access but does not necessarily delete data already stored in Drive or Calendar.

6. Security and limitations

The app relies on your browser, device security, HTTPS, and Google's security controls. No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure. Protect your device and Google account, and keep an independent backup of important information. The app does not guarantee that synchronization will always succeed or that data can always be recovered.

7. Children's privacy

The app is a general-purpose productivity tool and is not specifically directed at children. Do not use it to submit information you are not authorized to store.

8. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when app functionality or data handling changes. The “Last updated” date above indicates the latest revision. Continued use after a revision means you have had an opportunity to review the updated policy.

9. Contact

For questions about this policy or the app's data handling, contact the app maintainer through the contact details available on the app's GitHub repository: github.com/jayeshbhagat7/braindump.
